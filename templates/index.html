Why we verify

Verification keeps Devils to Devils safe for Sun Devils by ensuring members are current students, faculty, or staff. Your ASU credentials are never stored; we save the minimal data necessary to confirm eligibility.

Privacy reminders

  • ASU SSO is handled by the official ASU Identity Provider.
  • We store your ASURITE ID, email, and affiliations in accordance with community policy.
  • Discord OAuth only grants the permissions needed to join the server and manage your role.
