Devil2Devil fall 2025

Devil2Devil serves as ASU’s vibrant Discord community for admitted students enrolling in the fall 2025 semester. Whether you're an incoming first-year, transfer or master’s-level graduate student, from as close as central Phoenix to as far-flung as Hanoi, Vietnam, you can seamlessly connect with peers on Discord's engaging platform. Upon joining, you’ll be automatically sorted into channels based on your campus location, enabling you to form instant connections within your academic community. It’s a hub for forging friendships, finding potential roommates, engaging with current students about campus life and staying updated on all things ASU, ensuring a smooth transition before you set foot on campus in the fall.

Devil2Devil is open for engagement starting in January 2025 and is tailored for students attending in-person degree programs at ASU’s Downtown Phoenix, Polytechnic, Tempe and West Valley campuses, as well as LA Center.

Fostering a community in Discord

The Devil2Devil Discord server is integrated into ASU’s Student Hub, which hosts 500+ servers exclusively for ASU students. After joining Devil2Devil, you’ll smoothly transition to using Discord for classes, project teams and club communications once the semester starts.

Join Discord

Access Devil2Devil from your phone

Accessing the Devil2Devil Discord server from the Discord app on your phone is easy. Simply download the app, sign in to your account, click on the invite link you received via email or text message, and complete onboarding. The server will automatically be added to your account after you complete onboarding, and you can access it from the app or desktop at any time. Stay engaged with the ASU community, find roommates and access valuable resources on the go, all from your mobile device.
