Why we verify

Safety first for the Devils to Devils community

Verification keeps Devils to Devils safe for Sun Devils by ensuring members are current students, faculty, or staff. Your ASU credentials are never stored; we save the minimal data necessary to confirm eligibility and manage roles.

Need a refresher later? Return to this page anytime to reconnect or update your details.

Privacy reminders

  • Official SSO. ASU SSO is handled entirely by the university Identity Provider; we never see your password.
  • Minimal data. We store only your ASURITE ID, email, and affiliations required to validate community eligibility.
  • Scoped Discord access. OAuth permissions are limited to joining the server and managing the verified role.

Questions along the way?

Email d2d-support@asu.edu or message a moderator in Discord. We’re here to help every Sun Devil get connected.
